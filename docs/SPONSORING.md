# Sponsor Heliox OS

Heliox OS is an open-source, privacy-first desktop agent. Sponsorship helps pay
for the physical hardware and repeatable testing that software-only CI cannot
provide: microphones, cameras, accessibility devices, EEG acquisition
hardware, clean-machine release validation, and cross-platform test systems.

[Sponsor Heliox OS through GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/VyomKulshrestha)

## Current funding goal: live neural-input validation

The neural-intent software path already supports synthetic data, recorded
playback, BrainFlow devices, and local Lab Streaming Layer streams. Those paths
let contributors develop and test the safety boundary, but they do not prove
live physical EEG accuracy.

The first **$250** of designated sponsorship will fund:

- an entry-level EEG headset with access to its raw signal;
- delivery, import, electrode, and adapter costs;
- repeatable calibration and signal-quality experiments; and
- publication of latency, accuracy, false-trigger, and rejection results.

The next **$1,500** is a stretch goal for a multi-channel research setup and
comparison against the entry-level device. Final purchases will be documented
after checking current availability and total delivered cost.

Heliox will describe demonstrations accurately. Synthetic and recorded-data
runs will remain labelled as such, and the project will not claim live brain
control until a physical device has passed the documented hardware checklist.
Neural input cannot approve destructive actions or control physical equipment.

## Suggested sponsorship levels

These are voluntary support levels, not purchases or investments:

| Level | Amount | What it supports |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Supporter | $5 one time or $3/month | CI, documentation, and routine maintenance |
| Builder | $25 one time or $10/month | Accessibility and hardware-test consumables |
| Neural-input backer | $100 one time or $25/month | The live EEG validation goal |
| Lab sponsor | $250 one time or $100/month | A complete hardware-validation milestone |

Sponsors may optionally be acknowledged in project updates. Sponsorship never
buys security exceptions, private user data, approval bypasses, or control over
technical results.

## How funds and progress will be reported

For hardware-specific goals, project updates will report:

1. the device or service purchased and its purpose;
2. the relevant test protocol;
3. measured results, including failures and limitations; and
4. the code, documentation, or reproducible artifact produced.

General sponsorship may also support release infrastructure and development
time. It does not guarantee a delivery date, feature request, or commercial
return.

## Other ways to help

You can also help by contributing code, testing Heliox on real hardware,
sharing reproducible bug reports, lending a supported EEG device, or connecting
the project with an accessibility or neurotechnology laboratory.
